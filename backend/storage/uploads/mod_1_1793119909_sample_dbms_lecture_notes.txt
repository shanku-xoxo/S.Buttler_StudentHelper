DATABASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS — MODULE 2: RELATIONAL MODEL & NORMALIZATION

1. Relational Model Fundamentals:
A relational database consists of tables (relations). Each tuple represents a record.
Relational algebra operators include Selection (sigma), Projection (pi), Natural Join, and Division.

2. Normalization & Normal Forms:
- First Normal Form (1NF): All attributes must be atomic.
- Second Normal Form (2NF): Must be in 1NF and no non-prime attribute is partially dependent on any candidate key.
- Third Normal Form (3NF): Must be in 2NF and no non-prime attribute is transitively dependent on candidate key.
- Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF): For every functional dependency X -> Y, X must be a super key.

3. Transactions & Deadlock:
ACID properties guarantee reliability. Deadlock occurs when processes hold resources in a circular chain.
