B+ TREE INDEXING IN MODERN DATABASES

Introduction:
A B+ tree is an self-balancing tree data structure that maintains sorted data and allows searches, sequential access, insertions, and deletions in logarithmic time.

Properties:
- All leaves are at the exact same depth.
- Internal nodes store search-key values and pointers to child nodes.
- Leaf nodes are linked sequentially as a doubly-linked list for fast range scans.

Fan-out and Performance:
High fan-out reduces the tree height, typically resulting in 3 to 4 levels for millions of records.
